# Data and font notice

## Data

The CSV files in `data/` are author-derived aggregate outputs. They are not official government statistics and should not be represented as such.

Source statistics and metadata for the 2021–2024 descriptive analysis in `data/a3/`:

- 2021 Economic Census for Business Activity, e-Stat table `0004005689`  
  https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0004005689
- 2024 Economic Census for Business Frame, e-Stat table `0004040080`  
  https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0004040080
- Japan Standard Industrial Classification, 14th revision and crosswalk  
  https://www.e-stat.go.jp/classifications/terms/revisions/10/04
- e-Stat municipality boundary-change information  
  https://www.e-stat.go.jp/municipalities/cities

Source statistics for the 2021 cross-sectional model sensitivity in `data/phase1/`:

- 2021 Economic Census for Business Activity, e-Stat table `0004005687`  
  https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0004005687
- 2020 Population Census, e-Stat table `0003448299`  
  https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0003448299

e-Stat terms of use:

- https://www.e-stat.go.jp/terms-of-use

Last checked: 2026-08-24.

The repository reproduces article figures from already aggregated CSV files. It does not include the upstream e-Stat download, missing-cell proof, industrial-classification alignment, municipality aggregation, or full transition-matrix construction pipeline.
